### Overview
Foursquare’s enterprise location intelligence business relied on authoritative consumer trend studies, foot traffic indexes, and whitepapers to generate high-value sales conversations. However, enterprise executives at target retail and Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) brands rarely visited corporate blog archives. To intercept these decision-makers where they already spent their time, I shaped a multi-touch B2B paid social campaign across LinkedIn and Twitter.

Partnering directly with the enterprise sales enablement team, we mapped out a high-priority "wishlist" of executive titles—including CMOs, SVPs of Real Estate, and Heads of Consumer Insights at Fortune 500 retail and restaurant chains. I built modular ad templates with our design team and orchestrated multivariate copy testing. Within weeks, the campaign generated hundreds of inbound enterprise leads, with over 43% meeting the strict criteria for sales team outreach.

**My Role:** Campaign Strategy · Audience Architecture · Creative Direction · Copywriting · Performance Optimization

- **High-Value Lead Quality:** Rather than broad contact acquisition, 43% of inbound form submissions were verified executives and directors matching the enterprise sales team’s priority account tier.
- **Repeatable Template Engine:** Created a flexible visual asset library that allowed the marketing team to repackage 50+ page foot-traffic reports into bite-sized social cards in under 24 hours.
- **Cross-Platform Innovation:** Adapted dense analytical whitepapers into interactive Twitter polls and mini-quizzes, achieving superior engagement compared to static link cards.

### The Challenge
Location data can quickly feel dense and abstract. Previous lead generation relied on generic promotional posts linking to report download gates, resulting in low click-through rates and poor lead quality:

- **Audience Cynicism:** Busy retail executives ignore corporate self-promotion and standard vendor whitepaper pitches.
- **Channel-Specific Friction:** LinkedIn audiences respond to rigorous industry data cards, whereas Twitter users demand immediate interactive hooks.
- **Lead Quality Gap:** Outbound sales teams were frustrated by low-intent, non-buying contacts generated through broad display channels.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
Rather than asking prospects to download an unknown report, I led with the most surprising insights extracted directly from Foursquare's foot traffic data:

> **Proprietary Foot Traffic Data → Standalone Micro-Insights → Value-First Social Touchpoint → Report Download**

- **LinkedIn Thought Leadership:** Positioned the **Holiday Consumer Foot Traffic Report** with high-contrast data charts showing retail mall declines vs. off-price apparel surges.
- **Twitter Gamification:** Turned the **Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) Loyalty Index** into interactive quizzes (*"Can you guess which burger chain lost the most market share this quarter?"*), revealing the data upon interaction.

### Key Takeaway / Learning
B2B social campaigns succeed when they treat ad units as standalone editorial value rather than promotional billboards. Giving away high-value insights upfront builds immediate trust, ensuring that the leads who choose to download the full asset are already convinced of the product's authority.
