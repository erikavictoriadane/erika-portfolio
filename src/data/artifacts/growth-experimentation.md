### Background & Strategic Objectives
Faced with plateauing organic demo requests and escalating blended customer acquisition costs (CAC), the enterprise SaaS business required a unified growth framework. The objective was to replace disjointed ad hoc marketing tactics with a rigorous, hypotheses-driven experimentation engine that accelerated velocity from visitor awareness to enterprise contract close.

### Marketing Strategy & Execution
- **Data-Driven Conversion Funnel**: Overhauled top-of-funnel conversion architecture by introducing interactive ROI calculators, ungated diagnostic assessments, and modular product tour experiences.
- **Hypothesis-Driven Experimentation**: Instituted weekly growth sprints testing landing page messaging variants, high-intent lead magnet positioning, and dynamic pricing disclosures.
- **Behavioral Lifecycle Nurture**: Partnered with product teams to build event-triggered email sequences inside HubSpot and Customer.io, delivering contextual onboarding tips based on in-app usage thresholds.
- **Paid Channel Realignment**: Shifted performance budgets toward high-intent conversational LinkedIn thought-leader ads and Google Search intent clusters, drastically cutting budget leakage.

### Key Results & Commercial Impact
- Increased Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) to Sales Accepted Lead (SAL) velocity by **58%**.
- Drove a net **+165% expansion in enterprise sales pipeline** over three consecutive quarters.
- Decreased overall blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) by **31%** while scaling ad spend.
