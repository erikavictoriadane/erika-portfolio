### Overview
As Foursquare expanded its B2B suite across developer APIs, SDKs, and attribution products (Places API, Pilgrim SDK, TV Attribution, and foot traffic reports), the Performance Marketing team faced a major operational barrier: every new product campaign or whitepaper required custom engineering hours to build and publish dedicated web pages. This created multi-week launch bottlenecks and drained technical resources.

With enterprise lead generation established as a primary quarterly OKR, I took the initiative to design and launch Foursquare’s first modular landing page system inside Pardot, our marketing automation platform. Translating conversion optimization best practices into functional Balsamiq wireframes and detailed UX functional specifications, I managed the project from concept through design partnership and overseas frontend development, delivering an autonomous, high-converting template library for marketing.

**My Role:** Wireframing & UX Architecture · Technical Requirements · Creative Briefing · Pardot Integration · Cross-Functional Delivery

### The Challenge
Foursquare was releasing new products at a rapid pace, but the marketing infrastructure was fragmented:

- **Developer Sprints Overloaded:** Core engineers were prioritized on core platform features, leaving marketing requests backlogged for weeks.
- **Inconsistent Conversion Architecture:** Ad-hoc landing pages had disparate form fields, disjointed mobile viewports, and fragmented lead notifications.
- **Imminent Major Launches:** The impending rollout of Foursquare TV Attribution required immediate, conversion-ready web collateral that could not wait for standard development cycles.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
Rather than asking for single-page development exceptions, I architected a permanent system:

> **Campaign Friction → Reusable Information Architecture → Agile Pardot Modular System**

I designed two versatile archetypes to support the entire marketing funnel:

- **The Product Conversion Template:** Above-the-fold lead capture form, structured feature highlight cards, and customer proof badges (geared toward technical products like Places API and Pilgrim SDK).
- **The Editorial / Resource Template:** Long-form narrative hierarchy, executive quote callouts, and streamlined gated asset access (geared toward industry reports and newsletters).
