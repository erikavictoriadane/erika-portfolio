### Overview
The annual Fall Style Issue has long served as a signature editorial tentpole and commercial driver for InsideHook. In previous years, the campaign relied on a predictable recurring premise: women models donning menswear in a playful "who wore it better" shoot. However, reader feedback revealed the concept had grown tired, risking diminishing engagement for both the publication and its featured menswear sponsors.

To revitalize the tentpole, I guided a creative refresh of the narrative, reframing the photo shoot from a standard comparison gimmick into a witty, historically grounded exploration of menswear heritage and style evolution. To bring this story to life across paid channels, I paired a 64-variant creative testing methodology with Facebook's then-novel full-screen Canvas and carousel ad formats, driving high traffic to the issue at record-low cost-per-click rates.

**My Role:** Campaign Refresh · Creative Direction · Multivariate Ad Testing · Copywriting · Mobile Canvas Production

### The Challenge
Established brand traditions can easily become repetitive creative traps:

- **Audience Fatigue:** Direct subscriber feedback warned that the long-running menswear comparison trope felt dated and cliché.
- **Sponsor Delivery Requirements:** Commercial apparel brands required substantial impression volume and qualified shopping clicks to justify partnership commitments.
- **Mobile Screen Limits:** Standard static link previews failed to showcase the rich art direction and photography produced by the editorial team.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
We retained the beloved visual aesthetic while completely reinventing the storytelling purpose:

> **Audience Feedback on Trite Tropes → Creative Pivot (Fashion History Lesson) → 64-Ad Testing Matrix → Immersive Mobile Canvas**

Instead of a shallow modeling battle, the models guided readers through the historical origins of military trench coats, workwear denim, and tailoring. Across paid social, I structured 64 ad combinations testing image crops against cheeky, educational headlines to isolate the most compelling entry points.

### Key Takeaway / Learning
Refreshing a legacy brand franchise doesn't require abandoning what works—it requires listening to audience cues and elevating the substance. Pairing a smarter editorial angle with modern mobile-native ad formats revitalized an annual institution into an efficient growth driver.
