### The Challenge
Healthcare practices were hearing more about AI search, but what it actually meant for patient acquisition wasn't clear.

The opportunity was to make a fast-moving topic practical and accessible—educating prospective practices on evolving discovery while building interest in WebMD Care.

### Finding the Story
My research pointed to a simple, grounding insight:

> **AI is changing where and how discovery happens, so practices need to think differently about what visibility means today.**

Rather than focusing on AI itself, the messaging centered on what the shift meant for practices: patient discovery was evolving, and their visibility strategy needed to evolve with it.

> **Search is changing → Discovery is expanding → Visibility needs to evolve**

### Turning the Strategy Into Customer Education
I translated this positioning into **"7 Ways to Stay Visible as Patient Search Evolves"**, an educational guide helping practices understand the key factors shaping their online visibility.
