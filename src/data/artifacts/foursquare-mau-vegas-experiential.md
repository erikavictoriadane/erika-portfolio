### Overview
MAU (Mobile Apps Unlocked) in Las Vegas is the premier annual gathering of top mobile brand marketers, growth executives, and app developers. For Foursquare, MAU 2018 represented a vital commercial stage to reposition the company from a consumer check-in novelty to the premier enterprise location intelligence platform powering Fortune 500 apps and attribution campaigns.

Working on the ground in Las Vegas with the Growth Marketing, Enterprise Sales, and Business Development teams, I coordinated an end-to-end event marketing program. This encompassed on-site brand installations, executive meeting hospitality, bespoke branded collateral, VIP networking cocktail hours, and a high-profile main-stage thought leadership session co-presented alongside TouchTunes.

**My Role:** Event Marketing Strategy · On-Site Brand Production · Collateral & Swag Design · Executive Hospitality · Sales Enablement

### The Challenge
Industry mega-conferences present significant noise and distraction:

- **Perception Lag:** Many enterprise attendees still associated Foursquare primarily with historic consumer gamification (badges and mayors) rather than developer SDKs and multi-touch foot traffic attribution.
- **Competing Vendor Din:** Standing out amidst hundreds of enterprise tech vendors requires more than generic banners and throwaway pens.
- **Cross-Functional Coordination:** Managing on-site logistics, client hospitality, and collateral distribution in a fast-paced Las Vegas resort environment demands tight operational discipline.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
We structured the conference presence around meaningful executive engagement rather than passive foot traffic:

> **Main Stage Validation → Premium Executive Lounge → Curated VIP Hospitality → Deal Acceleration**

- **Proof Over Promotion:** Rather than presenting a generic sales pitch, our stage session featured our client TouchTunes demonstrating concrete business impact from our Places API.
- **High-Touch Hospitality:** We designed private branded lounge areas where business development leads could host private deal discussions over barista coffees and morning meals.
- **Thoughtful Brand Collateral:** Produced high-end, functional merchandise and printed developer guides that mobile engineers and product leads actually kept and brought back to their offices.
