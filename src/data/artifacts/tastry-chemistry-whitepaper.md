### Overview
To establish Tastry's authority among commercial winemakers—the primary target for the company’s analytical laboratory services—I developed a technical content initiative grounded in the science of winemaking. Collaborating with our customer success lead and internal laboratory scientists, I edited and designed **"Diving Into the Chemistry of Buttery Chardonnay"**, a technical whitepaper deconstructing diacetyl concentrations, malolactic fermentation, and sensory perception.

While the paper offered rigorous analytical insights, early distribution revealed a critical audience friction: busy winemakers refused to fill out multi-field landing page forms. Rather than forcing a traditional gated lead-generation model, I adapted the content into an open, high-density "Bulletpoint Breakdown" on the blog and featured it in our email newsletter. The test produced the highest click-through rate in company history and generated direct, organic inbound inquiries from commercial winemakers.

**My Role:** Editorial Strategy · Audience Research · Publication Design · Landing Page Architecture · Email Distribution

### The Challenge
Tastry’s core revenue relied on analytical laboratory services, yet our marketing up to that point had focused primarily on early-stage sales and retail apps. To attract production winemakers, we needed to prove our scientific bona fides at their level of technical rigor.

However, standard B2B marketing playbooks created real friction:

- **Time-Constrained Buyers:** Winemakers are constantly on the cellar floor and in the vineyard during harvest and blending seasons. They don't have patience for lengthy gatekeeping forms.
- **Zero Media Budget:** With no dedicated paid ad budget, lead generation had to rely entirely on owned channels, newsletter reach, and organic industry credibility.
- **The Gated Form Trap:** Qualitative user interviews confirmed that gating technical whitepapers caused target prospects to bounce before seeing the underlying value.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
Rather than defending an underperforming gate, I adapted our distribution to match how winemakers actually consume information:

> **Rigorous Scientific Research → Dense Whitepaper Publication → Ungated "Bulletpoint Breakdown"**

Instead of hiding the insights behind contact forms, I published the complete summary directly on our blog:
- Extracted key chemical thresholds (diacetyl ppm markers, yeast strains, temperature variance).
- Formatted findings into bold, easily scannable takeaways that could be read in two minutes.
- Maintained a clean link to the downloadable full technical report for those who wanted the complete laboratory dataset.

### Key Takeaway / Learning
Form gates often protect lead-count vanity metrics at the expense of genuine audience engagement. When marketing to specialized, skeptical technical professionals, leading with radical, ungated value builds the credibility that ultimately drives high-intent inbound demand.
