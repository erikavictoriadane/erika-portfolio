### Overview
The Grace Hopper Celebration (GHC) is the world’s largest gathering of women technologists, drawing tens of thousands of top software engineers, data scientists, and engineering leaders. To strengthen employer branding and build a qualified candidate pipeline for high-level technical roles, Foursquare’s recruiting team hosted an exclusive off-site breakfast event: the **"Fresh Start: Women in Tech Breakfast"**.

Facing a tight 2.5-week promotional window and stiff competition from every major tech titan on the ground, the talent acquisition team needed rapid marketing support to drive qualified candidate RSVPs. I developed and managed a targeted paid social acquisition campaign across Twitter, leveraging audience interest layering and empowering creative copy. The campaign generated 179 high-caliber RSVPs against an initial goal of 150, forcing registration to close early and establishing a waitlist for the event.

**My Role:** Campaign Strategy · Paid Social Acquisition · Copywriting · Audience Targeting · Registration Tracking

- **Exceeded Attendance Target:** Secured 179 confirmed RSVPs within 2.5 weeks, easily outpacing the venue capacity threshold of 150 attendees.
- **Overcapacity Demand:** The influx of candidate interest allowed the recruiting team to cap registrations ahead of schedule and establish an exclusive waitlist.
- **High-Intent Candidate Pipeline:** Connected Foursquare engineering executives with senior female developers and technical leaders in an intimate networking setting, yielding direct recruitment interviews.

### The Challenge
Competing for talent attention during major industry conferences is notoriously difficult:

- **Aggressive Competition:** Hundreds of enterprise tech giants (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) host simultaneous lavish networking receptions during GHC.
- **Short Runway:** With less than three weeks before conference kickoff, the campaign had to generate immediate conversion velocity without wasted spend.
- **Candidate Selectivity:** The event was designed specifically for qualified software engineers and technical practitioners, requiring precise targeting rather than broad student attendance.

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
I designed a fast-moving campaign focused on genuine career connection:

> **Conference Geotargeting & Tech Interests → Authentic Employer Branding Copy → Frictionless RSVP Page → Fully Booked Event**

- **Precision Paid Social:** Targeted women software engineers, computer science professionals, and confirmed Grace Hopper attendees using tech-stack keywords, developer affinities, and conference hashtag tracking.
- **Empathetic, Welcoming Positioning:** Positioned the breakfast as a restorative, high-value alternative to noisy conference halls—a relaxed morning space to discuss technical roadmaps and mentorship with female engineering leadership at Foursquare.
- **Active Pipeline Tracking:** Monitored daily conversion rates to pace spend efficiently, stopping spend immediately once capacity was surpassed.
