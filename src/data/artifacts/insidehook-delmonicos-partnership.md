### Overview
Delmonico’s Restaurant is an American culinary institution—celebrated as the birthplace of the Delmonico steak, Eggs Benedict, and Baked Alaska in lower Manhattan. When partnering with InsideHook for a sponsored culinary feature, the restaurant’s leadership wanted to reach a discerning, high-net-worth New York audience passionate about fine dining, heritage hospitality, and premium steaks.

To maximize the commercial impact of the partnership, I orchestrated a tailored audience architecture combining first-party newsletter reader engagement with fine-dining affinity data. The campaign drove high-intent traffic to our custom editorial landing page, where an embedded video showcase achieved an outstanding 80% full-completion rate. The campaign delighted Delmonico’s executive team so thoroughly that they invited InsideHook back for an exclusive, live-broadcast culinary special with their executive chef.

**My Role:** Audience Segmentation · Paid Media Strategy · Video Distribution · Client Partnership Management

### The Challenge
Luxury hospitality and heritage brand sponsorships require nuance:

- **High Discretion Threshold:** Promoting a $100+ fine-dining steakhouse requires reaching consumers with genuine discretionary spending power, not casual fast-casual diners.
- **Video Dwell Time Drop-off:** Video views on open social feeds are notoriously shallow (often counting after just 3 seconds). The goal was driving meaningful long-form viewing on-site.
- **Protecting Brand Heritage:** The creative had to honor Delmonico’s 180-year New York legacy without feeling stuffy or overly promotional.

![Prime Advice: Delmonico's Restaurant — Editorial Video Showcase & Campaign Art](/previews/insidehook-delmonicos-hero.png)

### The Strategy & Creative Approach
Rather than blasting broad regional demographics, I built a multi-layered precision audience architecture:

> **First-Party Editorial Signal → 2% Lookalike Layering → Fine-Dining Discretionary Targeting → Dedicated Dwell Time**

- **First-Party Behavioral Base:** Extracted a high-intent seed audience of InsideHook subscribers who consistently opened and clicked food & beverage editorial coverage.
- **Behavioral Lookalike Modeling:** Generated a refined 2% lookalike audience within the greater New York metropolitan area.
- **Affinity & Spending Filters:** Layered strict behavioral exclusions and qualifications, narrowing the pool to frequent diners at premier steakhouses with validated high-tier dining expenditure.

Audience precision matters far more than raw impression reach. By respecting the luxury positioning of a heritage partner and pairing first-party behavioral data with refined dining affinities, marketing delivered genuine brand engagement that deepened a commercial relationship.
