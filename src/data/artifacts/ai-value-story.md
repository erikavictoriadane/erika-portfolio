### Overview
As AI began changing how patients search for healthcare providers, WebMD Care faced a new positioning challenge: how do we communicate the value of a physician directory when search itself is evolving?

I researched changes in patient search behavior and developed a new messaging strategy that connected WebMD Care's value to the emerging AI-search landscape. I then translated that positioning into sales training and practical resources for customer-facing teams.

**My Role:** Research · Product Positioning · Messaging Strategy · Sales Enablement · Training

### The Challenge
AI search was changing the way patients discovered care, which challenged traditional ideas about what it meant for a healthcare practice to be "visible" online.

We needed to answer three questions:

- **What does provider visibility mean as patient search and discovery evolve?**
- **What does that change mean for healthcare practices?**
- **Where does WebMD's physician directory fit?**

The challenge was to turn a complicated topic into a story that was easy to understand and easy for Sales to use.

> **We couldn’t change the product, so the challenge was how to reframe the product’s value for a changing market.**

### Writing the Value Story
I researched AI-search trends and patient discovery behavior, then developed a narrative that moved from:

> **Market change → Customer impact → WebMD Care's role**

That became the foundation for **Selling Visibility in the AI Era**, a training presentation that gave Sales the context, messaging, and supporting evidence they needed to communicate WebMD Care's value amid changing search behavior.

Following the Sales rollout, I was asked to adapt the presentation for Customer Success and lead a second training for that team, extending the new positioning across both acquisition and retention conversations.

#### Reframing Directory Listings into Structured AI Signals
Rather than treating directory profiles as static online listings, the training reframed them as machine-readable structured feeds that supply authoritative signals directly to LLMs and search engines.
