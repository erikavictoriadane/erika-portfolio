### Overview
Acquiring customers is only part of the equation—keeping them engaged and helping them realize the ongoing return on their investment is essential to long-term business health. WebMD Care faced customer retention challenges with its core directory subscription product: Featured Profiles gave healthcare providers tools to increase online visibility, showcase their practice, and connect with prospective patients, but customers did not always take full advantage of those capabilities, and the commercial value they were receiving was not always immediately apparent.

To solve this retention challenge, I architected a comprehensive lifecycle marketing strategy designed to create meaningful engagement at every stage of the customer journey. By structuring communications around four foundational customer moments—**Educate**, **Inform**, **Recognize**, and **Improve**—we transitioned WebMD Care from a reactive churn-defense posture into an automated, value-reinforcing customer experience.

**My Role:** Lifecycle Marketing Strategy · Customer Journey Mapping · Email Automation Architecture · Educational Content Development · Cross-Functional Execution (Marketing, Product, Design & Engineering)

- **Resilient Multi-Month Engagement Retention:** Rather than experiencing the standard steep drop-off typical of onboarding sequences, open rates remained remarkably resilient over the 3-month cycle—launching at **45.4%** for Email 1, maintaining **39.5%–42.8%** across Emails 2–5, and concluding at **36.6%** for Email 6 across **3,577 delivered touchpoints**.
- **Measurable Educational Adoption:** The comprehensive Provider Success Guide generated **88 downloads all-time**, with **9.1% of enrolled providers** downloading the asset within 48 hours of receiving the concluding onboarding touchpoint.
- **Substantial Non-Enterprise Churn Reduction:** At launch in April 2026, non-enterprise customer churn had reached a peak of **73.0%**. Following the rollout of the automated onboarding and reporting touchpoints, churn dropped immediately to **45.5% in May** (▼ 27.5-point decrease) and reached a **series low of 40.9% by September**—a **32.1-percentage-point decrease** representing a **44% relative reduction**.
- **Accelerated Directory Growth:** Over the same April-to-September period, WebMD Care added **792 net active profiles (+12.3% active directory expansion)**, reversing earlier churn headwinds and stabilizing baseline recurring subscription revenue.

### The Challenge
Subscription directory products often suffer from an inherent visibility paradox:

- **Invisible Value Delivery:** Much of a Featured Profile's impact happens passively through search engine results, competitor profile impressions, and syndicated directories. Providers rarely log into their portal dashboards unless prompted, making ROI feel abstract or non-existent.
- **Tool Underutilization:** New customers frequently set up their accounts once and leave them static, failing to add high-converting photos, specialties, or recent patient reviews.
- **One-Size-Fits-None Outreach:** Generic newsletter blasts failed to address where a physician actually was in their lifecycle—whether they were an anxious new customer needing guidance, an established practice seeking proof of performance, or a lagging profile needing optimization.

> **Customers recognize product value when they understand how to use it, see what it is doing for them, and know what to do next.**

### The Strategic Framework: Four Customer Needs
To deliver the right message at the right moment, I organized the post-purchase experience into four distinct lifecycle pillars:

> **Educate (Onboarding) → Inform (Monthly Performance) → Recognize (Milestones) → Improve (Quality Optimization)**

Each communication pillar was designed to deliver relevant information at the exact moment of need, encourage high-value actions, and continually reinforce the commercial wisdom of the provider's investment.

### Educate: Helping New Customers Get Started
A successful onboarding experience goes far beyond welcoming new customers—it is about helping them quickly understand the tools they have purchased and showing them how to get the most out of their investment.

I developed the **Provider Success Series**, an automated six-email onboarding journey delivered over a three-month arc. The sequence introduces customers to key Featured Profile capabilities, explains how to interpret visibility metrics, and provides practical advice for strengthening their online presence. Crucially, send timing adapts dynamically based on engagement: providers who have not opened an email receive additional time before moving to the next touchpoint, allowing customers to progress at a personalized pace.

### Inform: Making Performance Data More Accessible
One of the primary drivers of subscription cancellation is that customers do not recognize the results they are receiving unless they actively search for them.

The **Monthly Profile Performance** email was designed to bring that critical ROI directly into the provider's inbox every month. Each personalized report delivers a clear snapshot of their Featured Profile's monthly impact, including total profile views, search impressions, competitive visibility, and direct patient actions such as phone calls and website clicks.

Month-over-month percentage comparisons provide immediate context, demonstrating how visibility is trending over time and offering a clear, frictionless invitation to explore their full analytics dashboard.

![Monthly Profile Performance Email — Delivering Actionable Visibility and Lead Metrics Directly to Inboxes](/previews/customer-moments-performance-email.png)

*The initiative required close cross-functional collaboration between Marketing, Product, Design, and Development to reliably translate complex database analytics into an engaging, customer-facing email experience.*

### Recognize: Celebrating Customer Milestones
Patient reviews play a decisive role in helping prospective patients evaluate healthcare providers—and they are an area where practices can take immediate action to build trust.

To encourage sustained engagement, I developed milestone communications recognizing providers as they reach meaningful review benchmarks. Rather than sending repetitive, nagging reminders asking doctors to collect reviews, these communications celebrate what practices have already accomplished and provide positive reinforcement:

- **10 Patient Reviews Milestone:** Celebrates the practice's first major social proof achievement, encouraging providers to link their profile in patient follow-up communications.

![Preferred Provider Review Milestones — 10 Patient Reviews Celebration Email](/previews/customer-moments-milestone-10.jpg)

- **25 Patient Reviews Milestone:** Recognizes sustained momentum and reinforces the practice's growing reputation among prospective patients.

- **50 Patient Reviews Milestone:** Celebrates an extraordinary practice achievement that places the provider in the top tier of directory credibility.

### Improve: Turning Profile Quality Into Actionable Guidance
Not every customer requires the same level of assistance. For providers whose profile quality score falls below benchmark thresholds, I developed a targeted communication designed to make profile optimization feel approachable rather than intimidating.

The **Low Quality Score Email** explains why profile completeness matters for search ranking and patient conversion, introduces simple steps practices can execute immediately, and links directly to educational video content and account management support.

![Low Quality Score Optimization Email — Actionable Recommendations to Rebuild Profile Strength](/previews/customer-moments-low-quality-score.jpg)

Rather than framing a low score as a punitive failure, the messaging treats it as an immediate commercial opportunity to capture more visibility and unlock greater value from their existing subscription.

#### Key Takeaway
Retention in recurring B2B subscriptions is won or lost in the post-purchase experience. By mapping customer communication to real-world behavioral needs—educating early, proving value consistently, celebrating accomplishments, and providing gentle, actionable guidance when accounts lag—we turned a passive directory listing into an active, high-retention marketing partnership.
