### Overview
Exclusive live performances gave listeners a reason to open RADIO.COM, and a reason to keep listening.

I led the app marketing strategy for RADIO.COM LIVE, using exclusive performances by artists including Coldplay and AWOLNATION to give people a compelling reason to open the app, engage, and start listening. I designed targeted campaigns to build anticipation, encourage opt-ins, and bring fans into the app when performances began.

*Brand context: RADIO.COM was the digital audio brand used when this campaign ran, before the company's 2021 rebrand to Audacy. Campaign visuals retain the original RADIO.COM identity rather than the later Audacy colors and design.*

**My Role:** App Marketing Strategy & Execution · Audience Segmentation · CRM & Push Messaging · In-App Experiences · Creative & CTA Testing · Cross-Channel Performance Analysis

### The Challenge
The challenge wasn't simply promoting a live performance. It was using the exclusivity and excitement of each show to motivate people to open RADIO.COM, return at the right time, and start a listening session. That meant connecting relevant audiences with timely, easy-to-act-on messages during a short broadcast window.

The events also varied in scale and audience. Coldplay was carried across 17 stations; AWOLNATION centered on 104.3 The Shark (WSFS). A broad, one-size-fits-all promotion wouldn't necessarily be the most effective use of our audience or channels.

RADIO.COM LIVE used exclusive artist performances as an app engagement strategy. By making the app a destination for live entertainment, we could encourage app opens and listening sessions while contributing to the platform's total listening hours. I owned the app marketing plan for each event, including audience segmentation, timing, in-app and push messaging, creative testing, and performance analysis. The repeatable program let me use what we learned from each performance to strengthen the next campaign.

### Building the Journey Around the Broadcast

#### Before: Find the Right Fans and Build Anticipation
I used listening behavior to shape who received event communications. For Coldplay, targeting included alternative, pop, and hot adult contemporary listeners, people who had previously listened to live events, and fans associated with participating stations. For AWOLNATION, the audience centered on recent alternative-rock listeners, prior live-event listeners, and 104.3 The Shark listeners and favorites.

Full-screen in-app messages introduced the events before showtime. Interested listeners could explore the announcement and, for Coldplay, opt in to a "Get Alert" reminder. We also accounted for negative responses: people who chose "No, thanks" were excluded from subsequent targeted messaging.

#### During: Turn Interest Into Listening
When each show began, the campaign shifted from awareness to action. Timed push notifications, in-app messages, homepage placements, and mobile-web experiences directed relevant audiences toward the live broadcast. For Coldplay, we used a pre-event alert pathway and a live landing page with "Listen" and "Watch" actions. For AWOLNATION, the live push targeted recent alternative listeners and previously interested users while excluding people who had declined.

Our event-time pushes converted attention into listening: the Coldplay message reached 75,306 devices, generated 4,756 opens, and recorded 871 KROQ listening-event conversions. AWOLNATION's reached 74,506 devices and recorded 588 listening conversions within the hour.

#### After: Learn What Actually Moved People
The work didn't end when the stream did. I examined campaign response, listening conversions, message placement, creative language, and how results varied by audience and geography to identify which tactics deserved to carry forward.

### What the Campaigns Taught Me

#### Make the Live Experience Unmistakable
For AWOLNATION, we tested "Listen Now" against "Listen Live" on in-app pop-up messaging during the broadcast. The "Listen Live" treatment generated 205 listening events from 664 sends (30.83%), compared with 193 listening events from 676 sends (28.55%) for "Listen Now." It was a directional signal, not proof of statistical significance, that emphasizing the event's immediacy could improve response.

#### Interest Matters More Than Raw Reach
Coldplay reached a broad station network, while AWOLNATION appealed to a more specific audience. The AWOLNATION pre-event in-app message received 571 "Tell me more" responses from 54,052 impressions, alongside a much larger number of "No, thanks" responses. That reinforced the importance of relevant targeting and respecting stated disinterest rather than sending every reminder to everyone.

#### Put Promotions Where the Audience Already Is
Coldplay's post-campaign analysis indicated that California stations experienced stronger listening growth than most East Coast participants, informing a recommendation to focus marketing more heavily in the market where a live event occurs. For AWOLNATION, an in-article app banner drew a 2.10% click-through rate (32 clicks from 1,525 impressions), outperforming the top/bottom and side-banner CTRs in that report, though the sample was too small for a firm placement conclusion.

### Campaign Performance
- **Coldplay (January 17, 2020):** 63K listening hours across 17 participating stations, +27% versus the previous four-week average. My event-time push drove 4,756 opens and 871 attributed KROQ listening-event conversions from 75,306 deliveries.
- **AWOLNATION (January 31, 2020):** 1,784 total-day WSFS listening hours, +27.79% year over year. My event-time push recorded 588 listening conversions, while two in-app CTA variants generated a combined 398 listening events.

Together, the campaigns demonstrate how I translated event marketing strategy into measurable listener action. The listening-hour lifts capture station-level performance during each broadcast (using different baselines); push and in-app conversions measure results attributed to the marketing touchpoints I managed. These measures serve different purposes and aren't additive.

---

### The Takeaway
RADIO.COM LIVE was an engagement strategy built around a simple insight: exclusive content gives people a reason to open an app. I translated that insight into coordinated audience journeys, from building anticipation and capturing interest to delivering a timely reminder and prompting a listening session. The campaigns combined entertainment, lifecycle messaging, and creative experimentation in service of app engagement and listening growth.
