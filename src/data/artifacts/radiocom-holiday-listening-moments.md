### Overview
Holiday listening was a reason to open RADIO.COM.

For the 2019 season, I built our app marketing around the moments when people wanted music most (traveling, shopping, decorating, and celebrating) and made it easier to discover the right station, whether they were new to the app or already listening.

I led the app marketing strategy and execution across paid acquisition, mobile-web-to-app journeys, in-app discovery, push, email, social, and streaming audio. My work connected acquisition to in-app engagement and listening, contributing to a holiday program that generated 4.8M listening hours across seasonal stations (+40% year over year). Along the way, our marketing drove measurable installs and engagement across multiple app touchpoints.

*Brand context: RADIO.COM was the digital audio brand used when this campaign ran, before the company's 2021 rebrand to Audacy. Campaign visuals retain the original RADIO.COM identity rather than the later Audacy colors and design.*

**My Role:** Integrated App Marketing Strategy · Cross-Channel Campaign Execution · Paid Acquisition · CRM & Lifecycle Messaging · Creative Testing · Performance Analysis

### The Challenge
Our overarching holiday objective was to increase total listening hours (TLH) by helping people find programming that fit their seasonal needs. From an app marketing perspective, that meant solving two related problems: bringing new listeners into the platform and making it easier for existing users to find something worth playing.

The campaign ran from November 22 through December 31, with messaging organized around three listening occasions: Thanksgiving and Black Friday; holiday shopping, baking, and atmosphere; and Christmas listening.

---

### Finding the Listening Moments
Rather than promoting the app in the abstract, I focused our mobile marketing around what people were already doing. Holiday road trips called for familiar music. Shopping and decorating created opportunities for background listening. As Christmas approached, the need for seasonal stations became even more immediate.

That gave us a simple organizing principle: make listening the invitation, and make the route to the right station as direct as possible.

---

### Building the Campaign Across the App Journey

#### Attracting New Listeners
Paid campaigns across Facebook, Google, and Apple Search Ads introduced RADIO.COM to people looking for holiday audio. I also used Branch Journeys to connect mobile web visitors with an app-install path. Streaming audio, email, and organic social extended the campaign across additional listener touchpoints.

The acquisition results showed where scale and efficiency came from. Google delivered 13,787 reported installs at a $2.46 CPI; Facebook's install-focused campaign delivered 2,403 at a more efficient $1.41 CPI, with one winning creative reaching $0.92 CPI. Branch Journeys generated 3,708 reported mobile-web-to-app installs, while Apple Search Ads delivered 251 at $8.99 CPI. I used those differences to identify where to scale and where to rethink spend.

#### Making Holiday Listening Easy to Find
Getting someone to install or open the app was only half the journey. I coordinated in-app landing pages, homepage banners, Braze in-app modals, and push notifications to help listeners find holiday stations when they were ready to play something. The reported Braze in-app messages generated 568,452 impressions and 169,381 clicks, a 29.8% click-through rate.

Those placements gave listeners multiple ways into seasonal programming: a homepage entry point, a timely in-app prompt, a curated station page, or a push notification tied to a station's switch to Christmas music or to a holiday-focused location.

### What the Campaigns Taught Me

#### Testing What Resonated
The creative findings were some of the most useful outcomes. Across our channel tests, bright whites, snowy imagery, and green treatments tended to outperform darker or red-heavy designs. For the in-app modals, green creative performed better than red across 18 of 22 station campaigns. In Branch Journeys, white lights and illustrated treatments were the strongest variations for clicks and installs.

#### Reduce the Distance to Listening
Facebook also surfaced a messaging lesson: "Listen Now" generally performed better than "Install Now." For Christmas Classics, a snow image without text achieved 109 iOS installs at $0.92 each, while a three-phone-screen execution delivered more installs at roughly $1.01 on iOS and $1.10 on Android.

The message was bigger than which color or image won. The best-performing creative often reduced the distance between a listener's immediate desire (hearing holiday music) and the action we wanted them to take.

#### What the Channels Taught Us
The channel analysis helped shape recommendations for what we would do differently next time. Branch Journeys outperformed traditional web banners as a mobile acquisition path, while in-app messaging delivered much stronger engagement than many other promotional placements. Homepage banners were a useful gateway to curated listening pages, and station buttons drew more interest than individual podcasts in the holiday landing-page experience.

Paid acquisition also had distinct roles by channel. Facebook was the most efficient of the reported paid-install channels, while Apple Search Ads was the most expensive. National interest-based Facebook campaigns appeared more cost-efficient than market-by-market targeting, suggesting we should reserve more localized promotion for channels such as digital audio.

Those findings turned the campaign recap into a practical set of next steps: invest in stronger app entry points, favor listening-first messaging, keep testing creative across placements, and direct acquisition dollars toward the channels that could scale efficiently.

### Campaign Results: Acquisition, Engagement & Listening
- **4.8M holiday-station listening hours (+40% YoY):** The broader listening outcome of the seasonal program. My campaign helped bring listeners into RADIO.COM and guide them toward relevant stations.
- **$1.41 Facebook CPI:** 2,403 reported installs from the install-focused campaign, with an individual winning creative as low as $0.92 CPI.
- **13,787 Google installs:** The largest reported paid-install volume in the campaign, at $2.46 CPI.
- **29.8% Braze in-app message CTR:** 169,381 reported clicks from 568,452 impressions, showing how strongly timely in-app creative engaged listeners.
- **3,708 Branch Journeys installs:** Reported mobile-web-to-app acquisition, extending the campaign beyond paid-install ads.

---

### The Takeaway
I built this campaign around a simple consumer insight: people weren't looking for another app to download; they wanted holiday music for a moment in their day. By carrying that insight through acquisition creative, mobile-web journeys, in-app discovery, and lifecycle messaging, I connected the path into RADIO.COM with the reason to stay and listen. The results gave us both a strong seasonal listening outcome and clear evidence of which channels, messages, and creative approaches drove app acquisition and engagement.
