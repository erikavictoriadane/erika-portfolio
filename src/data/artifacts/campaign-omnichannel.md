### Background & Strategic Objectives
Orígens Collective sought to enter a crowded premium lifestyle market without resorting to commodity promotional discounting. The challenge was building immediate brand resonance, establishing sustainable beauty credentials, and creating seamless continuity between experiential retail pop-ups and direct-to-consumer digital channels.

### Narrative-Led Creative Direction & Packaging
Built a thematic creative platform titled *"The Art of Balanced Living"*, emphasizing botanical transparency, sustainable craftsmanship, and sensory unboxing.

![Custom amber dropper bottles & sustainable embossed unboxing experience](/previews/campaign-packaging.jpg)

### Marketing Strategy & High-Impact OOH Execution
- **Integrated Omnichannel Roadmap**: Coordinated simultaneous launch beats across organic social, performance media, targeted PR giftings, and metropolitan flagship window displays.
- **Metropolitan Billboard Dominations**: Secured landmark OOH visual placements across SoHo New York and London West End to drive immediate credibility.

![High-impact SoHo metropolitan OOH billboard placement](/previews/campaign-billboard.jpg)

- **Audience Segmentation & Paid Acquisition**: Deployed dynamic creative optimization (DCO) across Meta, TikTok, and Pinterest, pairing top-of-funnel storytelling films with localized high-intent product carousels.
- **Experiential Retail Activation**: Curated sensory launch galleries in New York, London, and Tokyo featuring botanical installations and live founder masterclasses.

### Key Results & Commercial Impact
- Surpassed initial 90-day revenue goals by **240% within the first 28 days**.
- Generated **18.4M cross-platform organic impressions** and achieved a 4.2x ROAS across paid acquisition funnels.
- Earned feature profiles in *Vogue*, *Fast Company*, and *Monocle*, driving 38% organic referral traffic.
