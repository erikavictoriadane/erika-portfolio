### Overview
Across a year of Meta advertising, I kept coming back to the same question: do healthcare practices respond better to elaborate imagery or to a clear, direct message?

I tested text-first creative against image-led ads across recurring seasonal and evergreen campaign flights. When I connected those creative categories to closed-account and revenue data, the difference was striking: text-first ads delivered a 67% lower cost per closed account.

**My Role:** Paid Media Strategy · Creative Direction · Copywriting · Creative Testing · Salesforce Attribution · Budget Allocation

### The Challenge
Our Meta campaigns had to do more than collect inexpensive leads. They needed to help independent healthcare practices discover WebMD Care—and eventually become paying customers. I wanted a creative approach that could work across campaign seasons without sacrificing acquisition efficiency.

### Finding the Hypothesis
When I took over Meta advertising at WebMD Care, I started by analyzing the creative that was already running.

One ad stood out. While most featured healthcare imagery and designed graphics, the strongest performer was remarkably simple: a single headline on a plain white background with the WebMD Care logo.

That finding challenged my assumptions about what effective social advertising needed to look like.

> **What if a clear, direct message about attracting patients could outperform more elaborate creative?**

I used that insight to develop an ongoing creative-testing approach, comparing text-first ads against image-led variations across seasonal and evergreen campaigns.

Instead of treating that as a one-time holiday test, I compared these approaches across recurring campaign flights from October 2025 through September 2026. Across evergreen, holiday, spring, summer, and other campaign flights, I developed seasonal image-led executions alongside simpler text-first variations. This created an ongoing way to evaluate how different creative approaches performed as messaging, seasonality, and delivery conditions changed.

---

### A Year of Creative Experimentation
The creative approach ran across multiple campaign flights—not as one continuously controlled A/B test, but as repeated real-world comparisons between text-first and image-led advertising.

The annual analysis included 30 text-first and 31 image-led ad variants. I used the accompanying Salesforce attribution data to evaluate not only form fills but closed practices, annualized recurring revenue, and acquisition cost.

The 2025 holiday flight provides a particularly clear visual example: I compared benefit-led headlines such as "Attract More Patients in 2026" and "Patients Are Searching. Are You Findable?" in type-driven layouts versus provider-image executions with similar seasonal framing.

![Meta Ads Manager Dashboard — Holiday 2025 Campaign Flight Delivery & Lead Performance](/previews/paid-social-meta-ads-ui.png)

### Featured Example: Holiday 2025
In this holiday flight, 12 creative variants delivered approximately 912K impressions, 935 form fills, and a $47.31 blended cost per form fill. These figures describe the featured holiday campaign—not the full 12-month comparison.

### The Creative Insight

#### 01 / Make the Message the Visual
Text-first executions made the core promise the visual focus. Rather than assuming additional imagery would improve an ad, I began prioritizing how quickly the message could be understood.

#### 02 / Lead With the Customer's Goal
The question "Patients Are Searching. Are You Findable?" tied the ad directly to a practice's patient-acquisition problem.

#### 03 / Use the Season as Context
Across campaign flights, seasonal cues created variety and relevance. The stronger-performing text-first category showed that those cues didn't always need to dominate the composition.

### Connecting Creative to Closed Revenue
Meta's lead metrics alone couldn't tell me which creative approach was bringing in customers. By connecting the ad results to Salesforce attribution, I could compare closed accounts, recurring revenue, and the cost of acquiring each customer across the 12-month period.

![Salesforce Revenue Attribution & Closed Account Analysis Across Creative Variants](/previews/paid-social-salesforce-revenue-attribution.png)

In the featured holiday analysis, the two leading text-first variants were associated with 208 qualified leads, 35 closed practices, and $124,968 in annualized recurring revenue. The yearlong comparison gave a broader picture of the pattern:

| Creative Strategy | Variants | Media Spend | Form Fills | Closed Accounts | Attributed ARR | Cost / Closed Account | ARR / Spend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Text-First Creative** | 30 | $77,009.74 | 357 | 72 | $258,864 | $1,069.58 | **3.36×** |
| **Image-Led Creative** | 31 | $32,383.19 | 121 | 10 | $34,632 | $3,238.32 | 1.07× |

Across 30 text-first variants, $77,009.74 in spend generated 357 form fills and was associated with 72 closed accounts and $258,864 in annualized recurring revenue. The 31 image-led variants received $32,383.19 in spend, generated 121 form fills, and were associated with 10 closed accounts and $34,632 in annualized recurring revenue.

That translates to approximately **$1,070 spent per closed text-first account versus $3,238 per image-led account—a 67% difference in acquisition cost**. The reporting showed a stronger revenue-to-spend result for text-first ads, even after accounting for their larger media budget.

The overall social attribution audit also identified revenue outside this specific text-versus-image comparison, including legacy or uncategorized touchpoints. I kept those separate rather than folding them into the two creative groups.

- **$258,864 in Annualized Recurring Revenue:** Attributed directly to 30 text-first ad variants across the October 2025–September 2026 analysis window.
- **3.36× vs. 1.07× Media Return Multiple:** Annualized recurring revenue divided by media spend demonstrated that text-first ads delivered more than triple the spend in annualized value, compared to near break-even for image-led ads.
- **67% Lower Cost per Closed Account:** $1,069.58 text-first versus $3,238.32 image-led across the 12-month analysis.
- **72 Closed Practices Attributed:** Scaled delivery and high commercial intent drove 72 closed accounts from text-first ads, compared to 10 from image-led ads.
- **Holiday Flight Outperformance:** The scaled "Attract More Patients in 2026" text-first variant alone was associated with 28 closed accounts and $97.4K in annualized recurring revenue on approximately $30K ad spend.

### The Takeaway
The strongest creative isn't always the most elaborate. By comparing two creative approaches across a full year of campaigns—and measuring the results all the way through to closed revenue—I found a clearer basis for deciding which ideas deserved more investment.

*Reporting window: October 2025–September 2026. Holiday flight figures are one illustrative subset, not additive to the annual totals. Return multiples are calculated with annualized recurring revenue divided by ad spend.*
